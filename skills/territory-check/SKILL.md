# Territory Check

Answers one question: does this territory suck? Five questions, weighted. It tests the territory before anyone blames the rep.

## Inputs

Ask the five fixed questions in `reference.md`, in order, and accept only **yes**, **sort of**, or **no**. If the user describes the territory in prose, map what they said to each question, show the mapping, and ask only for what you cannot answer. Do not invent spend figures, account names, or history. Unknown is "no" or "sort of", and say which you used.

Say "territory", not "patch", in everything you write.

## Steps

1. Score exactly as in `reference.md`.
2. Find the weakest pillar (lowest answer; ties go to the heavier weight).
3. Give the verdict as an answer to "Does this territory suck?", with the proven count and the weakest pillar.
4. Give the fixed sentence and next line for the verdict.
5. List up to three moves, weakest first, for every pillar that was not a yes.
6. Offer the manager's questions for the weakest pillar (the Pressure Test).
7. End with the practical implication in one line: workable; thin, so every account needs a plan; mostly a territory problem, so take the sizing to your manager in month one; or nobody could hit this, so say so now with the math.

## Voice

Plain and direct. Speak to "you". Verdict first. If it is the territory, say so; if it is workable, say that the number is now on you. No motivational close, no referral to any service.
