# Comp Plan Check: reference

Ported from quotabird.com/comp-plan. Do not re-tune.

## The five questions, in order

| # | Pillar | Question | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CREDIT | Is it written down who gets credit for a deal, including partner, marketplace and split deals? | 24 |
| 2 | PAYOUT | Do you know when each commission gets paid, and does it show up within a quarter of the deal? | 18 |
| 3 | UPSIDE | Can you earn above your target without a cap or decelerator taking most of it? | 22 |
| 4 | CLAWBACK | Are clawbacks spelled out, with a time limit on them? | 16 |
| 5 | CHANGES | Does the plan say mid-year changes aren't retroactive, and that quota moves when territory does? | 20 |

Answer values: yes = 92, sort of = 50, no = 8.

## Scoring

```
total = round( Σ (value × weight) ÷ Σ weight )     (Σ weight = 100)
if any answer = no: total = min(total, 74)
```

Proven count: yes = 1, sort of = ½, no = 0, out of 5. Weakest pillar: the lowest answer; ties go to the heavier weight.

## Verdicts

| total | Label | Say first | Sentence | Next line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 75 | Clear | Yes. It's clear. | You know how it pays, when it pays, and what can change. | Keep the plan and your quota letter somewhere you can find them. |
| 55 to 74 | Mostly clear | Mostly. Get a few answers in writing. | Most of it is written down. A couple of things aren't. | Get the missing answers in writing before a big deal closes. |
| 35 to 54 | Unclear | Not yet. Too much is unwritten. | Too much of how you get paid is in somebody's head. | Ask for the written rules now, while nothing is riding on the answer. |
| < 35 | Red flags | No. Get answers before you count on it. | You can't tell how, when or whether you'll get paid. | Get answers in writing before you count on this plan, or before you sign it. |

## Moves (for pillars that were not a yes, weakest first, up to three)

- CREDIT: Ask for the crediting rules in writing, with one example deal worked through.
- PAYOUT: Get the payout schedule and put the next three paydays on your calendar.
- UPSIDE: Run your plan through Pay Check and look at the 125% and 150% rows.
- CLAWBACK: Ask what triggers a clawback and how long the window lasts. Write down the answer.
- CHANGES: Ask how mid-year changes are handled before one happens.

If everything was a yes: "Keep a copy of the plan, your quota letter and every change notice in one folder."

## Ask your manager (for the weakest pillar)

- CREDIT: Who gets credit when a partner, the marketplace or another rep is on the deal, and where is that written?
- PAYOUT: When does commission on a deal I close this month actually hit my paycheck?
- UPSIDE: What happens to my rate above 100%, and is there a cap anywhere in the plan?
- CLAWBACK: What can be clawed back, for how long, and why?
- CHANGES: If my territory or quota changes mid-year, what happens to deals I already worked?
